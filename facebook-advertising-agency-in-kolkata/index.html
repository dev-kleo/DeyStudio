A Facebook advertising agency in Kolkata plans, launches and manages paid ads on Facebook and Instagram for your business. Kolkata agencies publish starting fees from about ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 a month. Your ad spend is paid to Meta on top of that, and 18% GST is usually added to the fee. Agencies suggest at least ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 a month of ad spend to get usable data, and competitive sectors such as real estate need more.

If you want a quote from us, see our page on Facebook and Instagram ads management.

Quick answer

  • What it is: an agency builds audiences and ads, runs the campaigns on Meta's platform, sets up tracking and reports what each enquiry or sale cost.
  • Management fee: published starting prices run from about ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 a month. Fees vary with the number of campaigns and how much reporting you get.
  • Ad spend: paid to Meta from your own ad account, separate from the fee. Agencies suggest at least ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 a month to start.
  • Tax: 18% GST is normally added to agency fees. Ask whether a quoted price includes it.
  • Time to results: first data in days, steadier results after about three to eight weeks of testing.
  • Guarantees: no honest agency can promise a number of leads or sales.

What does a Facebook advertising agency in Kolkata do?

The agency handles the work between your budget and a customer's enquiry. In practice that means:

  • Choosing the goal: form leads, WhatsApp messages, store visits or online sales
  • Building audiences by location, age, interests and past visitors to your site
  • Writing the ad text and preparing images or short videos
  • Setting up the Meta pixel and, where it fits, the Conversions API so every enquiry is counted
  • Watching spend, testing new versions of the ads and pausing what does not work
  • Sending a report you can read without a marketing degree

Agencies in Kolkata describe the same core list. The difference between them is how many campaigns the fee covers, how often they report, and whether the ad account sits in your name.

How much does Facebook advertising cost in Kolkata?

There are two separate costs. Keep them apart when you compare quotes.

  1. The management fee goes to the agency.
  2. The ad spend goes to Meta.

Management fees

Published starting prices on Kolkata agency sites range from about ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 a month. Some charge a higher first month for setup. One agency quotes ₹8,000 for the first month and ₹5,000 a month after. Several publish three or four tiers where the lowest covers one campaign and the upper tiers add more campaigns, more ad versions, retargeting and weekly reports.

Directory listings go lower, with some offers from ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a month. Treat those with care. At that price an agency can only afford a template campaign and little creative work.

Agency fees normally attract 18% GST, and at least one Kolkata agency says so on its price page. Ask for the price with GST included.

Ad spend

Agencies give these starting budgets for Kolkata businesses:

One Kolkata agency puts the average cost per click in India at ₹3 to ₹20. That is an agency's own estimate, and your cost will depend on your sector, your offer and your ad quality. A tight budget and a vague offer produce few clicks and no useful data. A clear offer to a nearby audience gives you the most information per rupee.

What the fee should include

Compare agencies on scope as well as price. A fair fee should say in writing:

  • How many campaigns and ad sets it covers
  • How many ad versions are made each month
  • Whether tracking setup (pixel, Conversions API, UTM links) is included
  • How often you get a report, and what it shows
  • Whether the ad account is created in your name

How long do Facebook ads take to work?

Agencies quote different timelines. First data usually shows within three to fifteen days, because Meta needs a few days to learn who responds. Steadier results tend to come after about three to eight weeks, once the agency has tested several audiences and ad versions.

Plan a test of at least eight weeks before you judge the channel. Stopping in the first week means you pay for the learning phase and never see the results that follow it.

Which Kolkata businesses do well with Facebook ads?

Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who are not searching yet, so they suit businesses with something visual or a local audience. Agencies in the city most often list restaurants, salons, coaching and education, real estate, interior design, ecommerce and D2C brands, and local services.

Some categories carry extra rules. Dental and medical clinics must follow professional advertising rules, which we cover in our guide to an advertising agency for a dental clinic in Kolkata. If you run a restaurant, your website matters as much as the ad, so read how to build a restaurant website in Kolkata.

Target by area

You can aim ads at a radius around your premises or at named neighbourhoods. Agency pages mention Salt Lake, New Town, Park Street, Dum Dum, Rajarhat and EM Bypass as examples. A shop with walk-in customers may use a radius of a few kilometres (agency pages describe 1 to 10 km for local businesses). A business that delivers across the city can use a wider area.

Match the language and the season

Test Bengali, English and Hindi versions of the same offer and keep the one your customers respond to. Festival periods such as Poila Boishakh and Durga Puja also change how people shop, so plan offers and budgets for them ahead of time. This is general advice, not a figure from any study, so test it on your own account.

Facebook ads or Google Ads first?

Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for what you sell, so it suits urgent needs such as a plumber or a clinic. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who may not be searching yet, so they suit discovery and offers. Many businesses start with one channel and add the other once tracking works. How to choose a Facebook advertising agency in Kolkata

Ask these before you pay anyone:

  1. Will the ad account be in my name, and can I leave with it?
  2. What exactly does the monthly fee include, and is GST included in the quote?
  3. How will you count enquiries? Ask to see the pixel or Conversions API events firing.
  4. Where do clicks land? A slow or unclear page wastes the ad spend.
  5. Is there a lock-in or notice period? Many agency pages do not state one.
  6. Who will manage the account, and how often will I get a report?
  7. Can you show an example report and a campaign for a similar business?

Warning signs

  • A promise of a set number of leads or a fixed cost per lead
  • A fee so low that it cannot cover creative work
  • The agency owns the ad account or will not share access
  • No tracking, or reports that only show likes and reach
  • Pressure to start with a large ad budget before any test

What to fix before you spend

Ads send people to a page. If that page is slow, hides the phone number or does not say what to do next, the ad money is wasted. Fix the landing page before you scale spend. Our guides on why a website gets visitors but no customers and on web development in Kolkata cover the usual problems.

Common mistakes

  1. Boosting posts without a goal, an audience or a landing page
  2. Running one audience and one ad, so nothing can be compared
  3. Judging results in the first week
  4. Skipping conversion tracking
  5. Counting likes and reach instead of enquiries and sales

Frequently asked questions

What does a Facebook advertising agency in Kolkata do?

It builds audiences and ads, runs the campaigns on Meta's platform, sets up conversion tracking and reports the cost of each enquiry or sale. Some agencies also build the landing page and write the ad text.

How much does a Facebook advertising agency in Kolkata charge?

Published starting fees range from about ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 a month, and bigger scopes cost more. Ad spend is paid to Meta separately, and 18% GST is usually added to the fee.

How much should I spend on Facebook ads in Kolkata?

Agencies suggest ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 a month for a small local test and ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 for steady lead generation. Competitive sectors such as real estate often need ₹30,000 or more.

Who pays Meta for the ads?

You do. The ad spend is charged to your own ad account, separate from the agency's management fee.

How long do Facebook ads take to work?

First data usually appears within days. Steadier results often take three to eight weeks of testing, so plan an eight-week test before you judge.

Do Facebook ads work for small businesses in Kolkata?

Yes, when the offer is clear and the audience is local. Small budgets work for a tight test, because you can target a radius or named neighbourhoods around your premises.

Can a Facebook advertising agency guarantee leads or sales?

No. Results depend on your offer, your website, your price and your competition. An agency that promises a fixed number of leads is a warning sign.

Do I need a website to run Facebook ads?

Not always. Ads can send people to a lead form, a WhatsApp chat or an Instagram profile. A good landing page usually lowers the cost per enquiry, so it helps.
