Website design and development for small businesses

Dey Studio designs and builds fast, clear websites that turn visitors into enquiries. You get a fixed price in writing, a delivery date and support after launch.

What we build

Business websites

Five to ten pages that load fast on a phone, say clearly what you sell, and make it easy to call, message on WhatsApp or send an enquiry.

Landing pages

One focused page for a Google Ads or Facebook ads campaign, built to turn clicks into enquiries.

Website redesigns

We keep your domain and content, replace the outdated design, and fix speed, mobile layout and search basics.

Custom web apps

Booking systems, customer portals, dashboards and internal tools when a standard website is not enough. If you want to sell products online, see our ecommerce website development page.

Website design and development prices

These are starting prices in Indian rupees. The final quote depends on the number of pages, features and content, and we send it in writing before any work begins.

  • Landing page: ₹8,000 to ₹20,000, ready in about 3 to 7 days
  • Business website of 5 to 10 pages: ₹20,000 to ₹60,000, about 2 to 3 weeks
  • Online shop: from ₹50,000, about 4 to 6 weeks
  • Custom web app: from ₹1,50,000
  • Monthly maintenance: from ₹2,000, covering updates, backups, security checks and small changes

How a project works

  1. Talk. You tell us what you sell and what the site should do, and we reply with a fixed quote.
  2. Plan and build. We agree the pages, write the structure and build the site, sharing progress as we go.
  3. Go live with tracking. We launch the site with call, form and WhatsApp tracking so you can see which visits turn into enquiries.
  4. Improve. We review the results and fix what holds back enquiries.

Platforms we use

We build on WordPress and WooCommerce, Shopify, fast static sites and custom JavaScript apps. We recommend the simplest option that fits your budget and your plans for growth.

What every site includes

Fast loading, a mobile-friendly layout, a sitemap, clean page titles and structured data, so Google and AI tools can read your business details. Your domain and content are registered in your name, and you receive the files when the project ends.
