Most small business owners who say "my website doesn't work" have a site that loads and looks fine. The problem is that it never asks a visitor to do anything, or asks in a way that is easy to ignore.

## 1. Nobody knows what you do in five seconds

A visitor decides quickly. If the top of your page does not say what you sell, who it is for and what to do next, they leave. Write one plain sentence, such as "We repair laptops in Leeds, same day", and put it where people look first.

## 2. There is no obvious next step

Every page needs one main action: call, message or book. Hiding it in a footer or a menu costs enquiries. Use a button with a clear label and repeat it further down the page.

## 3. The site is slow on a phone

Most visitors arrive on a phone. Large uncompressed images and heavy scripts make pages slow, and slow pages lose people before they read a word. Test your site on mobile data, not office wifi.

## 4. You rely on word of mouth only

A good site still needs people to find it. Search and ads send people who are already looking for what you sell. Google Ads is the fastest way to test demand, and a well-built page is what turns that spend into enquiries.

## 5. There is no proof

Visitors want a reason to trust you. Real photos, real customer comments and clear prices help more than slogans. If you do not have these yet, start collecting them now.
