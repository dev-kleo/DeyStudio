The cost of an ecommerce website in Kolkata depends on how many products you sell, which payment and shipping tools you connect, and how much of the design is built from scratch. ## What does ecommerce website development include?

Ecommerce development means building a website where customers can browse products, add them to a cart, pay online and get an order confirmation. A working store has these parts:

- A product catalogue with categories, filters, photos and variants such as size or colour
- A cart and checkout that works on a phone
- Payment gateways, usually UPI, cards, net banking and wallets
- Shipping and order tracking
- An admin panel where you add products and manage orders
- Basic SEO setup so Google can read your product pages

Design, hosting, security and post-launch support are often priced separately, so ask for each line in writing.

## How much does an ecommerce website cost in Kolkata?

Quotes in Kolkata vary widely, because the same word "store" can mean very different projects. The scope falls into three broad types:

| Type of store | What you usually get | Cost level |
|---|---|---|
| Template store | A ready-made theme, your products, a payment gateway | Lowest |
| Custom small or mid-size store | Custom design, tested checkout, shipping and payment integrations, SEO setup | Mid |
| Large or multi-vendor marketplace | Vendor dashboards, custom features, heavy integrations | Highest |

Two quotes for "an ecommerce website" are only comparable if the scope is the same. Ask every developer for a fixed quote with a written scope, and compare the feature lists before you compare the totals.

What moves the cost:

- The number of products and variants
- Custom design versus a theme
- The number of integrations, such as payment gateways, courier partners, accounting software and WhatsApp
- Features such as subscriptions, wholesale pricing, product bundles or a loyalty programme
- Moving products over from an old site or a spreadsheet
- Ongoing hosting, maintenance and support

## Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom build?

The platform decides your monthly costs, how easily you can change things yourself, and how far the store can grow.

Shopify is the fastest way to launch. It hosts the store, handles security and updates, and has a large app store. You pay a monthly subscription, and some apps add their own monthly fees. It suits sellers who want to launch quickly and manage the store without a developer.

WooCommerce runs on WordPress. The software is free, but you pay for hosting, premium plugins and maintenance. It gives you more control over design and data and suits sellers who already use WordPress or want fewer recurring platform fees.

A custom build makes sense for unusual requirements: a multi-vendor marketplace, complex B2B pricing, or an integration with software you already run. It costs the most and takes the longest, and it needs a developer to maintain it.

A simple way to decide: choose Shopify if speed and low maintenance matter most, WooCommerce if you want ownership and flexibility on a moderate budget, and custom only when neither can do the job.

## What does an Indian online store need?

Many templates are designed for Western shoppers. A store that sells to customers in Kolkata and the rest of India should handle these from day one:

- UPI and local payment options. Most Indian shoppers expect UPI, and many still prefer cash on delivery. Check which gateways your developer has integrated before, for example Razorpay, PayU or Cashfree.
- GST invoices. Orders should generate invoices with the correct tax details, and your accountant should be able to export them.
- Courier integration. Connect an aggregator or courier partner so you can print labels and show tracking links without copying addresses by hand.
- Cash on delivery and returns. COD brings return-to-origin risk. Decide your policy on COD limits, pincode checks and return windows before launch.
- Mobile speed. Most traffic to Indian stores comes from phones, often on mid-range devices and mobile data. A heavy theme loses buyers at the first page.
- Bengali and English content. If you sell to local customers, Bengali product descriptions and support details can help.
- WhatsApp contact. Many buyers want to ask a question before paying. A WhatsApp button on product and checkout pages shortens that gap.

## How long does it take to build an ecommerce website?

One Kolkata agency states that a Shopify project typically takes three to six weeks, depending on complexity and customisation. That matches what most small and mid-size stores need:

- Week 1: scope, platform choice, product data and brand assets
- Weeks 2 and 3: design and store setup
- Week 4: payment, shipping and tax integrations
- Weeks 5 and 6: testing, SEO setup, fixes and launch

Delays usually come from the seller, not the developer. Product photos, descriptions and price lists that arrive late are the most common cause. Prepare these in a spreadsheet before the project starts.

## How do you choose an ecommerce developer in Kolkata?

Most agency pages describe themselves as leading or trusted, so those words tell you nothing. Check these instead:

1. Ask for live stores they built, and place a test order yourself. Check how fast the pages load on your phone and whether checkout works.
2. Ask who owns the store, the domain and the hosting account. They should be in your name.
3. Ask what is included after launch: bug fixes, security updates, backups and support hours.
4. Ask for a fixed quote with a list of features, rather than an hourly rate with no cap.
5. Ask how they handle SEO on product and category pages, such as titles, descriptions, schema markup and a sitemap.
6. Ask which payment gateways and couriers they have already integrated for Indian stores.
7. Talk to one past client if they allow it.

A developer who answers these clearly and puts the answers in the contract is worth more than one with the lowest price.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does ecommerce website development cost in Kolkata?

It depends on the scope. Template-based stores cost the least, custom stores cost more, and marketplaces with vendor features cost the most. The main factors are product count, integrations, custom design and ongoing support. A written, fixed-scope quote is the only reliable way to compare developers.

### Is Shopify or WooCommerce better for a Kolkata business?

Shopify is faster to launch and needs less maintenance, with a monthly fee. WooCommerce gives more control and fewer platform fees, but you manage hosting and updates. Choose by how much time you want to spend on technical upkeep.

### How long does it take to build an online store?

Most small and mid-size stores take three to six weeks. Large catalogues, custom features and late product content add time.

### Can I accept UPI and cash on delivery?

Yes. Payment gateways such as Razorpay, PayU and Cashfree support UPI, cards and net banking, and most platforms can enable cash on delivery. Confirm the setup and fees with your developer before launch.

### Do I need GST registration to start an online store?

That depends on your turnover and what you sell. Ask your chartered accountant before launch, then ask your developer to set up invoices to match.

### Who owns the website after it is built?

You should. Ask for the domain, hosting, store admin and source files to be registered in your name before the project begins.

### Will my store show up on Google?

A new store needs correct page titles, product descriptions, a sitemap and fast pages, then time to be indexed. Developers can set up the technical side. Rankings also depend on content, links and competition.
