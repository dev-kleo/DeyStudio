## What is a creative advertising agency?

A creative advertising agency is a business that develops advertising concepts and produces the finished work: copy, design, photography, video and the plan for where it runs. The word "creative" means the idea and its execution come first. A media-buying firm, by contrast, only places ads that someone else made.

Most agencies you will meet in Kolkata do some of both. A studio might write the campaign line, shoot the reel, build the landing page and then run the Google and Meta campaigns that send people to it.

| Service | What you receive | Where it runs |

Agencies also use three shorthand terms. ATL (above the line) means mass media such as TV, radio and newspapers. BTL (below the line) means direct and local activity such as leaflets, in-store displays and events. OOH (out of home) means any ad people see outside the house, such as hoardings, bus shelters and metro branding.

## How is advertising in Kolkata different from other Indian cities?

Four local factors change how a campaign should be built.

Language is the first. Many Kolkata customers respond differently to copy written in Bengali than to a translation of English copy, and a lot of businesses run both. If your customers are Bengali speakers, ask who on the agency team writes in Bengali and whether you can see samples.

The festival calendar is the second. Durga Puja is the biggest retail season in the city, and Poila Baisakh and Kali Puja matter for many sectors. Campaigns usually need planning and creative sign-off weeks before the season starts, because ad costs rise when many brands compete for the same audience.

The third is the business mix. Retail, education, healthcare and real estate are common advertisers, and a cluster of software and startup companies has grown around Sector V and New Town. A restaurant on Park Street and a SaaS company in Sector V need very different campaigns, so an agency's sector experience matters more than its size.

The fourth is the way people see ads. Hoardings, newspapers and neighbourhood print are still part of daily life, and so are Instagram and Google. Most campaigns that work in Kolkata use both, with the same message adapted to each.

Two points save buyers the most money.

First, the agency fee and the ad spend are separate. If an agency quotes ₹20,000 a month, that is what the agency keeps. The budget that goes to Google or Meta is extra. Noir & Blanco puts the starter tier for small local businesses at ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 in fees plus ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 in ad spend. Decide your total monthly number first, then split it between fee and spend.

Second, what drives the quote is scope. The number of platforms, the amount of new creative each month, whether video is included, and how detailed the reporting needs to be all move the price. upGrowth notes that when a retainer looks high next to the ad spend, creative production is usually the reason, and it suggests asking the agency to price that line separately.

Indian agencies use three pricing models. A monthly retainer is the most common and suits ongoing work. A project fee suits a launch, a rebrand or a festival campaign. Performance-linked pricing ties part of the fee to leads or sales, and it works best when you can measure those cleanly.

## How do you choose a creative advertising agency in Kolkata?

Work through these seven checks before you sign.

1. Ask for named work. A useful case study names the client, states the goal and gives a number: leads, sales, cost per lead. If an agency can only show unsigned testimonials, you have no proof yet.
2. Get the fee and the ad spend written separately, and ask for the creative production cost on its own line.
3. Ask who writes the Bengali copy, if Bengali speakers are your customers, and ask to see samples.
4. Confirm who owns the ad accounts, the website and the design files. The answer should be you.
5. Agree on the metrics before launch. Cost per lead, return on ad spend, calls and store visits are all measurable. "Brand buzz" is not.
6. Ask to meet the people who will do the work, including whoever writes your copy and whoever runs your ad accounts.
7. Read the exit terms: notice period, and how logins and files are handed back.

Be wary of guaranteed rankings, guaranteed returns, "100% client satisfaction" claims, and any agency that will not discuss price until after a long call.

## How long does it take for advertising to show results?

Paid search and paid social campaigns can produce their first enquiries within days of going live, but they usually need several weeks of testing before the cost per lead settles. SEO and brand building take months. A good agency tells you which of these timelines applies to your plan before you start, so you can judge it fairly.

## How do you measure whether an agency is working?

Tie each goal to one number you can check yourself.

| Goal | Metric | Where to see it |
|---|---|---|
| More enquiries | Cost per lead | Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, your CRM |
| More online sales | Return on ad spend | The ad platform and your sales records |
| More awareness | Reach and branded search volume | Meta Ads Manager, Google Search Console |
| A better website | Conversion rate | Google Analytics |

Ask for read-only access to the ad accounts so you can look at these numbers without waiting for a monthly report.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a creative advertising agency?

A creative advertising agency develops advertising ideas and produces the finished work, including copy, design, video and campaign plans. Many also run paid campaigns on Google and Meta. The creative part means the concept comes first, and the media plan is built around it.

### How much does an advertising agency in Kolkata cost?

Small and mid-sized businesses commonly pay between ₹10,000 and ₹1,00,000 a month in agency fees, with retainers often starting around ₹25,000 for a full service. Ad spend is billed separately. Large national campaigns and network agencies cost considerably more.

### What is the difference between an advertising agency and a digital marketing agency?

An advertising agency focuses on the campaign idea and its creative execution across media, including print, outdoor and video. A digital marketing agency focuses on online channels such as SEO, social media and paid search. Many Kolkata agencies now do both, so ask which one leads their work.

### Do I need an agency that writes in Bengali?

Only if your customers read Bengali. If they do, copy written natively in Bengali often reads more naturally than a translation, and many Kolkata businesses run campaigns in Bengali and English at once. Ask to see Bengali samples before you hire.

### How long before I see results from an agency?

Paid campaigns can bring first enquiries within days of launch and usually need several weeks of testing to stabilise. SEO and brand awareness take months. Ask the agency to state the expected timeline for your plan in writing.

### Should I hire a freelancer or an agency?

A freelancer fits a single task, such as a logo or one platform. Published 2026 benchmarks put freelancer social media handling at ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 a month. An agency or studio fits work that needs several skills together, such as design, a website and ads, with one team accountable.

### What are ATL, BTL and OOH advertising?

ATL (above the line) is mass-media advertising such as TV, radio and newspapers. BTL (below the line) is direct and local promotion such as leaflets, in-store displays and events. OOH (out of home) is any outdoor ad, such as a hoarding, a bus shelter or metro branding.

### Can a small business afford a creative advertising agency in Kolkata?

Yes. Small starter packages for a single platform begin around ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 a month in agency fees, plus an ad budget. A smaller scope done well usually beats a large scope done thinly, so begin with the one channel where your customers already look.

### How do I check an agency before hiring?

Ask for named case studies with a goal and a result, read their live ads and website, request read-only access to ad accounts on any current client work you are allowed to see, and confirm who owns the accounts and files after the contract ends.
