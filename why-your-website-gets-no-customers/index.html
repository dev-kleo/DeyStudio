Visitors but No Customers? 5 Website Fixes

12 November 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026 · 3 min read

Five fixable reasons a small business website brings in traffic but no enquiries, and what to change first.

  2. 2. There is no obvious next step

  7. Frequently asked questions

Most small business owners who say "my website doesn't work" have a site that loads and looks fine. The problem is that it never asks a visitor to do anything, or asks in a way that is easy to ignore.

1. Nobody knows what you do in five seconds

A visitor decides quickly. If the top of your page does not say what you sell, who it is for and what to do next, they leave. Write one plain sentence, such as "We repair laptops in Leeds, same day", and put it where people look first.

2. There is no obvious next step

Every page needs one main action: call, message or book. Hiding it in a footer or a menu costs enquiries. Use a button with a clear label and repeat it further down the page.

3. The site is slow on a phone

Many visitors arrive on a phone. Large uncompressed images and heavy scripts make pages slow, and slow pages lose people before they read a word. Test your site on mobile data, not office wifi. If you are not sure how old your site is, our free domain age checker shows when the domain was registered.

4. You rely on word of mouth only

A good site still needs people to find it. Search and ads send people who are already looking for what you sell. Google Ads is the fastest way to test demand, and a well-built page is what turns that spend into enquiries.

5. There is no proof

Visitors want a reason to trust you. Real photos, real customer comments and clear prices help more than slogans. If you do not have these yet, start collecting them now.

What to fix first

Fix the headline and the main button before anything else. They cost little and change results fastest. Frequently asked questions

Why does my website get visitors but no enquiries?

Usually the visitor cannot tell what you do within a few seconds, has no clear next step, or finds the page slow on a phone. Fix the headline and the main button first, because they cost little and change results fastest.

How do I know if my contact form works?

Fill it in yourself from your phone, then check the inbox and the spam folder of the address that should receive it. A form that never sends loses every lead without any sign that something is wrong. Our longer guide, why is my website not generating leads, covers tracking and follow-up as well.

Should I run ads if my website gets no customers?

Fix the page first. Ads send visitors, but they cannot make a confusing page convert, so money spent on a weak page is wasted. Once the headline, button and speed are right, a small Google Ads test shows whether people who are looking for what you sell will enquire.

How long should I wait before changing my website?

Fix obvious faults such as a broken form or a missing phone number straight away. For changes to wording and layout, give each one a few weeks of visits so one quiet week does not mislead you.
