Advertising Company in Kolkata: Types, Costs and How to Choose

An advertising company in Kolkata plans and buys the clicks, newspaper columns or hoarding sites that put your business in front of customers. Some companies work only online. Others sell only print and outdoor space, and a few cover both. Pick one by who you need to reach and how you will count the result.

Published prices vary a lot because the services differ. Kolkata agency listings start Google Ads packages at about ₹4,999 to ₹15,000 a month, and a month on a large hoarding in a busy area costs several times that. On digital ads, the ad spend goes to Google or Meta and the agency's fee comes on top. 18% GST is usually added to the fee.

If you want us to run digital ads for you, see our pages on Google Ads management and Facebook and Instagram ads management.

Quick answer

  • What it is: a company that plans campaigns and books or buys the ad space, whether that is a search click, a newspaper column or a roadside hoarding.
  • Digital fees: Kolkata sites publish starting prices from about ₹4,999 to ₹15,000 a month. Guides covering India as a whole put Google Ads management at ₹20,000 to ₹1,00,000 a month, so a low quote usually means a small scope.
  • Hoardings: one buyer's guide estimates ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 a month for a small or medium site in a prominent spot, and ₹60,000 to ₹1,50,000 for a large site in a busy commercial area.
  • Newspapers: priced per square centimetre, and published figures for the same paper differ by several times. Ask for the current rate card in writing.
  • Results: digital ads can be counted enquiry by enquiry. Print and outdoor are harder to trace to a sale.
  • Guarantees: no honest company can promise a number of leads or sales.

Which kind of advertising company do you need?

Digital advertising agencies

They run Google Ads, Facebook and Instagram ads and, where available, ChatGPT ads. Many also build the pages the ads send people to. Every click and enquiry can be counted, so you can see what each one cost. See our Facebook advertising agency guide for how fees and ad spend work on Meta.

Media-buying agencies for print and outdoor

They book newspaper space, hoardings, transit branding and legal notices. One Kolkata media-buying agency describes bookings across more than 200 publications, and lists metro, bus and tram branding among its outdoor options. These companies suit announcements, recruitment ads and legal notices, where a public record or wide local visibility is the point.

Creative and branding agencies

They make logos, campaign ideas, films and design. Agency listings in the city describe creative advertising, branding, print solutions and logo design as separate services from media buying.

Many companies say they do all of it. Ask which work they do in house and which they pass to another firm.

Directories can help you build a shortlist. Pick an Agency lists 537 advertising agencies in Kolkata, and Semrush and Qoruz publish city lists too. Read the reviews and ask for examples before you contact anyone.

What does an advertising company in Kolkata cost?

Keep three costs apart when you compare quotes: the company's fee, the money spent on the ads or space, and tax.

Digital ads

Kolkata sites publish starting prices that range widely. An IndiaMART listing offers Google Ads at ₹4,999 a month. Other Kolkata pages start at ₹10,000 and ₹15,000 a month. One of them says its ad budget should be at least ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 a month, depending on the industry. National guides quote Google Ads management at ₹20,000 to ₹1,00,000 a month. The low end usually covers one campaign with limited reporting.

One India guide says your ad spend should be at least three times the management fee. If the fee is ₹15,000, that means starting from ₹45,000 of ad spend. Smaller local tests run on less, so check what your market needs before you take that rule as given.

Tax adds to the bill. A Google Ads guide shows that a ₹50,000 month of ad spend costs ₹59,000 after 18% GST, before any agency fee.

Hoardings and outdoor

A buyer's guide for Kolkata estimates ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 a month for a small or medium hoarding in a prominent location, and ₹60,000 to ₹1,50,000 for larger ones in busy commercial areas. It also says rates rise in peak seasons and festivals. Printing and mounting are extra: sites listed on Media Ant show printing at ₹10 to ₹11 per square foot and mounting at ₹5 to ₹6 per square foot. Other sources quote higher monthly figures, so treat any single number as a starting point and ask for a site-specific quote.

Newspapers

Display ads are priced per square centimetre. Published figures disagree. For Anandabazar Patrika, one agency lists about ₹450 per square centimetre for inside black and white pages, while Media Ant lists ₹1,014 for one edition and ₹4,298 for another. Media Ant also lists minimum sizes of 20 square centimetres on inside pages and 240 on the front page. Because the same paper shows such different numbers, ask the company for the paper's current rate card and the discount it offers on it.

How long before advertising works?

Digital ads show first data within days. Steadier results usually take several weeks of testing, so plan about eight weeks before you judge a channel. Print and outdoor campaigns run for a fixed period, and the effect is hard to separate from everything else happening in your business. If you use them, give each one its own phone number or offer code so you can tell where enquiries come from. This is general advice, not a figure from any study.

How to choose an advertising company in Kolkata

Ask these before you pay anyone:

  1. Which of these services do you run yourselves, and which do you pass to someone else?
  2. What does the fee include, and is GST in the quote?
  3. Will the ad account be in my name, and can I leave with it?
  4. How will you count enquiries? Ask to see tracking events firing.
  5. Is there a lock-in or notice period? Many price pages do not say.
  6. Who manages my account, and how often will I get a report?
  7. Can you show an example report and a campaign for a similar business?

Warning signs

  • A promise of a set number of leads or a fixed cost per lead
  • A fee so low it cannot pay for creative work
  • The company owns the ad account or will not share access
  • Reports that show likes and reach but not enquiries
  • Pressure to commit to a large budget before any test

Check your website before you spend

Ads send people to a page. If that page is slow, hides the phone number or does not say what to do next, the money is wasted. Fix the page first. Our guide on why a website gets visitors but no customers covers the usual problems, and our page on web development in Kolkata explains what we build.

If your business has special advertising rules, read the guide for your field first. Our article on an advertising agency for a dental clinic in Kolkata covers one example.

Common mistakes

  1. Buying a hoarding or a newspaper slot with no way to tell what it produced
  2. Boosting a post without a goal, an audience or a landing page
  3. Judging digital ads after the first week
  4. Skipping conversion tracking
  5. Letting the agency open the ad account in its own name

Frequently asked questions

What does an advertising company in Kolkata do?

It plans campaigns and buys or books the ad space for them. Depending on the company, that means Google and Facebook ads, newspaper and hoarding bookings, or creative work such as logos and films.

How much does an advertising company in Kolkata charge?

Digital ad management starts at about ₹4,999 to ₹15,000 a month on Kolkata price pages and runs to ₹1,00,000 a month in national guides, with ad spend paid separately. Print and outdoor are priced by the space, from a few hundred rupees for a small classified to lakhs for a full newspaper page or a large hoarding site. 18% GST is usually added.

Is an advertising company the same as a digital marketing agency?

Not always. Many digital marketing agencies call themselves advertising companies, but some advertising companies only sell print and outdoor space. Check the list of services before you contact them.

Which suits a small business, digital ads or newspapers and hoardings?

It depends on who you want to reach. Digital ads can start with a small, local test and show what each enquiry cost. Hoardings and newspapers suit announcements and local visibility, but they are harder to measure.

Who pays Google or Meta for the ads?

You do. The ad spend is charged to your own ad account, separate from the company's fee.

How long do ads take to work?

Digital ads show first data within days, and steadier results often take several weeks. Plan about eight weeks of testing before you judge a channel.

Can an advertising company guarantee leads or sales?

No. Results depend on your offer, your website, your price and your competition. A promise of a fixed number of leads is a warning sign.

How do I check that an advertising company in Kolkata is genuine?

Ask for examples from similar clients, a GST invoice and a written scope. Read reviews on more than one directory, and confirm the ad account will be in your name.
