Questions owners ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After our first chat I send a fixed price in writing, so there are no surprises. Ad budgets are separate and always go straight to Google, Meta or OpenAI from your own account.

How long does a website take?

A typical small business site takes a few weeks. I give you an exact timeline before we start.

Who owns the website and the ad accounts?

You do. The domain, the code and the ad accounts are all in your name. There is no lock-in and no subscription to me.

Can you promise more customers?

Nobody honest can promise a number. What I do promise is a site and ads built around enquiries, tracking so you see every lead, and fixes when something is not working.

Do I need all four services?

No. Many businesses start with the website, or with one ad channel. We pick what fits your goal and your budget.
