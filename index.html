Questions owners ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After our first chat we send a fixed price in writing, so there are no surprises. Ad budgets are separate and always go straight to Google, Meta or OpenAI from your own account.

How long does a website take?

A typical small business site takes a few weeks. We give you an exact timeline before we start.

Who owns the website and the ad accounts?

You do. The domain, the code and the ad accounts are all in your name. There is no lock-in and no subscription to us.

Can you promise more customers?

Nobody honest can promise a number. What we do promise is a site and ads built around enquiries, tracking so you see every lead, and fixes when something is not working.

Do I need every service?

No. Many businesses start with the website, or with one ad channel. We pick what fits your goal and your budget.

Are you a ChatGPT ads agency in India?

Yes. Dey Studio is an advertising agency with offices in Kolkata and Moscow. We run ChatGPT ads, Google Ads and Facebook and Instagram ads for small businesses across India, and we build the websites those ads send people to.
