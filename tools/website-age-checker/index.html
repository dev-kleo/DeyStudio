Website age checker

Enter any domain to see how old it is, when it expires, who registered it and when it first appeared on the Internet Archive.

How it works

We look up the domain's public registration record (RDAP) for its creation date, and the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine for its earliest saved snapshot. Domain age is counted from the registration date.

Is domain age the same as website age?

Not always. A domain can be registered long before a site goes live, or expire and be registered again by a new owner. The first Wayback snapshot shows when real content first appeared.

Does domain age affect SEO?

Age alone is not a ranking factor you can rely on. Older domains tend to have more links and history, but content quality, links and technical health matter far more.

Why is there no registration date for my domain?

Some country-code registries do not publish creation dates through RDAP. In that case we show the earliest Wayback snapshot instead, which gives a minimum age.
