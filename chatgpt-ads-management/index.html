ChatGPT ads management

Dey Studio sets up and manages ChatGPT ads for businesses in countries where they are available, starting with a small test, working tracking and a landing page that converts.

What ChatGPT ads are

ChatGPT ads are labelled sponsored placements shown below ChatGPT answers. In India they reach logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans, and the minimum budget is ₹725 a day for rupee-billed accounts. OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. Availability and rules differ by country, so we check before planning a campaign.

What we do

Check that your business is eligible

Alcohol, tobacco, gambling and political content are barred, and outside the US, financial, health and legal services ads are generally prohibited. We tell you before you spend if your category is not allowed.

Tracking and landing page

We set up conversion tracking and make sure the page the ad sends people to loads fast and has one clear action.

Ad text and topic hints

We write the ad and choose the conversation topics you want to appear beside, then review which ones bring enquiries.

Testing and reporting

We start with one offer and a small budget, then compare your cost per enquiry with Google Ads and Facebook ads before recommending more spend.

Who ChatGPT ads suit

Businesses whose customers ask long, specific questions before they buy, such as comparing options or planning a purchase. The channel is new, and early US data shows weaker revenue per visit than Google search ads, so we recommend a measured test and not a large budget.

What it costs

Ad spend is charged by OpenAI, and we recommend keeping the ad account and billing in your name. Our management fee is a fixed amount quoted in writing before work starts.

Frequently asked questions

Are ChatGPT ads available in India?

Yes. OpenAI launched ChatGPT ads in India on 27 August 2026, with a minimum daily budget of ₹725 for rupee-billed accounts. Availability and rules differ by country, so we check before planning a campaign.

Can any business run ChatGPT ads?

No. Alcohol, tobacco, gambling and political content are barred, and outside the US, financial, health and legal services ads are generally prohibited. We tell you before you spend if your category is not allowed.

Do ChatGPT ads make ChatGPT recommend my business?

No. OpenAI says ads do not influence ChatGPT answers. The ad appears in a labelled block below the answer.

Should I start with a test?

Yes. We start with one offer, one landing page, working tracking and a small budget, then compare your cost per enquiry with other channels.
