Facebook and Instagram ads management

Dey Studio runs Facebook and Instagram ads that bring enquiries, WhatsApp messages and sales. We build the audiences and creative, set up tracking, and report in plain language.

What we do

Campaign goals

We start from what you need: leads from a form, messages on WhatsApp, or sales from an online shop, and build the campaign around it.

Audiences

We target by location, interests and behaviour, and test narrow audiences against broad ones to see which brings enquiries at a lower cost.

Ad creative and text

We write the ad text and prepare images and short videos, using your own photos and offers so the ads look like your business.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

These ads open a chat with your business. They suit shops, restaurants, clinics and service businesses that sell by conversation.

Tracking and reporting

We install the Meta pixel and, where it fits, the Conversions API, then report enquiries, messages or sales against your spend.

Who Facebook and Instagram ads suit

Businesses with a visual product or a local audience, and businesses whose customers are not searching yet. Restaurants, salons, fashion and home brands, coaching and local services often start here.

What it costs

Meta charges your ad spend to your own ad account, and you set the budget. Our management fee is a fixed amount quoted in writing before work starts.

Facebook ads or Google Ads first?

Google Ads reaches people already searching for what you sell. Facebook and Instagram ads reach people who may not be searching yet. Many businesses start with one and add the other once tracking works. See our Google Ads management page for the other side.
