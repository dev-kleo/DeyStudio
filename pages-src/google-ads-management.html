Google Ads management for small businesses

Dey Studio sets up and runs Google Ads for businesses that want more calls, enquiries and sales. You own the account, you set the budget, and we report what each enquiry costs.

What we do

Account setup and keyword research

We find the searches your customers use, group them into campaigns and exclude the ones that waste money.

Ad copy and landing pages

We write ads that match what people search for, and make sure the page they land on is fast, clear and easy to act on. If it is not, we fix it or build a new landing page.

Conversion tracking

We track calls, form submissions, WhatsApp clicks and sales, so results come from real enquiries and not just clicks.

Ongoing optimisation and reports

We review search terms, adjust bids and budgets, pause what does not work, and send you a report you can read without a marketing degree.

Who Google Ads suits

Businesses whose customers already search for what they sell: local services, shops, clinics, restaurants, training providers and B2B suppliers. If nobody searches for your product yet, Facebook and Instagram ads may be a better first step.

What it costs

Google charges your ad spend to your own account, and you set the monthly budget. Our management fee is a fixed amount quoted in writing before work starts.

What we do not promise

Nobody can honestly promise a number of customers. We promise clear tracking, honest reporting, and changes based on your data.
