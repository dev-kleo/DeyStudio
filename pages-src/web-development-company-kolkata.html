How to choose a web development company in Kolkata

Almost every agency calls itself the best, so the claim tells you nothing. These checks tell you more.

  • A real address and a way to reach a person. Check that the address exists and that someone replies.
  • Prices in rupees. If a company will not give even a range, expect surprises later.
  • Numbers that agree with each other. If the years in business or review count change from page to page, ask why.
  • Work you can open. Ask for live sites, not screenshots, and check how fast they load on your phone.
  • Reviews you can check. Look for reviews on Google or an independent platform, not only quotes on the company's own site.
  • Who owns what. You should own your domain, content and files when the project ends.
  • What happens after launch. Ask who fixes problems and what it costs.
