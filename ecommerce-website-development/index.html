Ecommerce website development

Dey Studio builds online shops on Shopify and WooCommerce with UPI and card payments, shipping and tax set up, and product pages made to be found on Google. Online shops start at ₹50,000.

What we build

Shopify and WooCommerce shops

A shop with categories, product pages, a cart and checkout, set up so you can add products and manage orders yourself.

Payments by UPI and card

We connect a payment gateway such as Razorpay so customers can pay by UPI and card. Each gateway charges a fee per payment, so check the rate before you choose.

Shipping and tax

We set up delivery areas, shipping charges and tax so orders are priced correctly at checkout.

Product pages that rank

Clear titles, descriptions, images and structured data help your products appear in search results.

Price and timeline

Online shops start at ₹50,000 and take about 4 to 6 weeks. The final price depends on the number of products, the payment and shipping setup, and the design. You get a fixed quote in writing before work starts. Monthly maintenance starts at ₹2,000.

Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify is hosted and quick to launch, with a monthly fee paid to Shopify. WooCommerce runs on WordPress and gives more control, but you manage hosting and updates. We recommend the one that fits your budget, your number of products and your plans for growth.

What we need from you to start

  • Your products, prices and photos
  • Delivery areas and shipping rules
  • Your GST and business details
  • Your logo and brand colours, if you have them

Frequently asked questions

How much does an online shop cost?

An online shop starts at ₹50,000. The price depends on the number of products, payment and shipping setup, and design. You get a fixed written quote before work starts.

How long does it take to build an online shop?

About 4 to 6 weeks. The main delays are product photos, descriptions and prices arriving late.

Should I choose Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify is hosted and quick to launch, with a monthly fee paid to Shopify. WooCommerce runs on WordPress and gives more control, but you manage hosting and updates. We recommend the one that fits your budget and plans.

Can customers pay by UPI and card?

Yes. We connect payment gateways such as Razorpay so customers can pay by UPI and card. Each gateway charges a fee per payment, so check the rate before you choose.

Can I add products myself?

Yes. Both platforms have an admin area where you add products, change prices and manage orders without a developer.
